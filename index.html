ROOM 28 · GROUP 1

The Mystery Lamp

Mia and Tama opened the old storeroom. Dust floated through the light.

Behind a box, Mia found a small golden lamp. Tama rubbed it with his sleeve.

The lamp began to glow. A soft voice whispered, “Choose carefully.”

Mia and Tama stared at each other. What should they do next?

Talk about it: Who found the lamp? Where was it? What changed? How did they feel? What do you predict?
